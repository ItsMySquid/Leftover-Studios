Who we are

Leftover Studio is a thirteen-person development team working on our first original 3D title. We come from different disciplines — art, design, programming, production — but share one goal: ship a game we're proud of.

We work like a real studio: dedicated roles, sprint-based production and a single creative vision. Everything you see — from concept art to code — is made in-house by the team.
