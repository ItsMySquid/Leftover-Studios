The studio

We are Leftover Studio

Thirteen people. One game called Merchants. One shared obsession with the open skies.

Who we are

Leftover Studio is a thirteen-person development team working on our first original 3D title. We come from different disciplines — art, design, programming, production — but share one goal: ship a game we're proud of.

We work like a real studio: dedicated roles, sprint-based production and a single creative vision. Everything you see — from concept art to code — is made in-house by the team.

The project

MERCHANTS is our debut title — an open-world trading game set among floating islands, drifting rocks and vast skies. You pilot a balloon-powered ship from island to island, buying low and selling high, while cargo weight and shifting weather shape every journey. You navigate by maps and landmarks — but out here, the maps don't always tell the truth.
