About the game

PROJECT: LEFTOVER is our first title as a studio — a 3D game currently in active development by our thirteen-person team. The art direction, world and mechanics are being shaped right now, and we're documenting the process as we go.

We can't reveal everything yet, but we're building something we're genuinely proud of. Follow us on Instagram for devlogs, concept art and behind-the-scenes looks as the game comes together.
