About the game

MERCHANTS is an open-world trading game set among floating islands, drifting rocks and vast skies. You'll captain a balloon-powered ship from port to port, buying goods where they're cheap and selling them where they're wanted.

Every journey is shaped by the weather and the weight in your hold — a heavy cargo earns more but slows you down, and a windy sky can turn a safe route into a gamble. Navigate by map and landmark, learn the rhythms of the sky, and build your fortune one crossing at a time.

What to expect

Balloon-powered ships

Simple, weighty flying — your balloon does the lifting while you steer, trim and dock, every landing earned by hand.

Trading system

Buy low, sell high — prices shift from island to island, rewarding pilots who find the routes worth flying.

Cargo weight

A heavy hold is a slow ship — stack your profits high and feel every crate tug at the ropes.

Living skies

Calm mornings and windy afternoons rewrite every route — read the weather before you cast off.

Maps & landmarks

Not every map shows the world quite right — navigate by landmark and learn the sky yourself.

A growing world

Unique islands to explore, each with its own markets, people and secrets — and new horizons always on the way.
